# Sobre — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Mark (mark.c@gorocky.ph) — a young professional in the Philippines managing personal finances across five banks/e-wallets (Maya, MariBank, GoTyme, Tonik, GCash) plus a credit card. Primary context: logging expenses on a phone at point of purchase; weekly/payday review on desktop. Single user today, possibly shared with a partner later.

### Use Cases
Envelope budgeting: payday allocation (₱18,962/cutoff across 16 envelopes in home accounts), fast expense logging with payee + optional receipt items, credit-card debt tracked per envelope ("which account do I pull from to pay the card?"), recurring-bill tick-off, per-account and per-payee history.

### Brand Personality
Warm, playful, trustworthy — ":3 energy" grounded by money-green calm. The mascot is an envelope with peeking eyes (sobre = envelope in Tagalog). References the Otter logo vibe (chunky hand-drawn strokes, lowercase cozy serif) and Istorima-style springy logo animation.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Palette: **Pine + Amber duo** — pine `#2F6F4F`/`#1E4633` primary, amber `#FFB80A` accent (logo tile, bill chips), paper `#F6F6F4` bg, ink `#1A1D24` text. Money: red out `#C0392B`, green in. Light theme only (for now).
- Type: Fraunces Black for wordmark + page titles; Geist for UI/body/numbers.
- Real bank-brand card gradients on account surfaces (BPI red, GoTyme navy-teal, Tonik `#7657F8`, MariBank orange, Maya black/green, GCash blue) — single source in `src/lib/cardStyles.ts`.
- Paper Shaders (Mesh/Simplex/Static Mesh/Warp, user-selectable in Setup → Appearance) animate brand surfaces.
- Sharp-ish corners (`--radius: 0.25rem`), generous CTAs (44px+), shadcn/ui + Radix as the component base.

### Design Principles
1. The ledger is truth — every view derives from transactions; never a manually-synced duplicate.
2. Fast on the phone — logging an expense should beat opening a bank app.
3. Brand color belongs to the banks on their cards, and to pine/amber everywhere else.
4. Playful accents, calm surfaces — the mascot winks; the numbers stay sober.
5. Red = money out / warnings only; green = money in / primary actions.
